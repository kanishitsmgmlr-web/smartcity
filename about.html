Who We Are

Smart City Infra Developers provides high-standard plots, layouts & townships. We give utmost priority to our customer’s satisfaction and never compromise on quality. We understand your desires, thus we build hopes, beauty and comfort.

Smart City Infra Developers is a name that has been trusted over the years, owing to the many lives we have transformed across Karnataka — from Kotegangur, Virupinakoppa and Sri RamPura to Nidige, Purle, Bommanakatte and Davangere District.

We work as complete land builders and developers — we acquire land with crystal-clear titles, plan and develop gated plotted layouts, and build the infrastructure a community needs: black-top roads, water supply, three-phase electricity, street lights, parks and wide approach roads. Every plot we deliver is freehold, ready for registration and ready to construct, with unbeatable affordable pricing and flexible payment options.

“To set international standards, create new benchmarks, and develop state-of-the-art buildings and townships — using the latest technologies and adhering to industry best practices — with a view to forging enduring relationships with customers and stakeholders alike. Our teamwork, planning and timely execution will shine through as the key to our success.”

Our Vision — shaping landmark, lasting communities across Shivamogga that families are proud to call home.
