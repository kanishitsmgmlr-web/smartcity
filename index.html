FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about plots, titles, loans and bookings.

What plot sizes are available at SmartCity layouts?

We offer plotted sites in 30x40 (1200 sq ft), 30x50 (1500 sq ft) and 40x60 (2400 sq ft) across layouts like Pushpa Layout, Manohar Layout, Maruthi Residency, Sri Sai Residency, Sharadeesh Layout and ShriShaila Township.

Are the plots freehold with clear title?

Yes. Every SmartCity plot is freehold with a crystal-clear title, ready for registration. Our documentation is verified and transparent before any purchase.

Can I buy a plot on instalments or with a bank loan?

Absolutely. We offer flexible instalment options and full support for bank loan assistance on all ready-to-construct layouts.

What amenities are provided in each layout?

Layouts include wide roads, parks and greenery, street lights, water and electricity connections, and are fully ready to construct.

Where is the SmartCity office located?

We are at 1st floor, BK Nayak Arcade, Vandana Bakery & Cycle Loka upstairs, Savalanga main Road, Shivamogga, Karnataka 577201. Call +91 9620 585890 to book a site visit.

How do I book a plot or schedule a site visit?

Use the Book Online form on this website or call +91 9620 585890. Site visits are free and our sales team confirms appointments within 24 hours.
